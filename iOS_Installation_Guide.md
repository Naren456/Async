# How to Install ASync on your iPhone (No 7-Day Expirations!)

Because ASync is not on the App Store yet, you will need to "sideload" the app onto your iPhone using a computer. 

We highly recommend using **AltStore**. Once set up, AltStore will automatically refresh the app in the background over your home Wi-Fi, meaning **the app will never expire and you won't have to plug your phone in every 7 days!**

---

## 🛠️ Requirements
- An iPhone and a lightning/USB-C cable
- A Windows or Mac computer
- Your Apple ID email and password (used securely to tell Apple you trust the app)
- Both your iPhone and Computer must be connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Download the App File
1. Download the `ASync-Unsigned-IPA.zip` file provided to you.
2. Unzip/Extract the file on your computer to reveal the `app.ipa` file inside.
3. Keep this `app.ipa` file handy on your computer.

### 2. Install AltServer on your Computer
1. On your computer, go to [altstore.io](https://altstore.io/).
2. Scroll to the bottom and download **AltServer** for macOS or Windows.
3. Install AltServer and open it. 
> **Note for Windows Users:** AltServer requires you to have the non-Microsoft Store versions of iTunes and iCloud installed. The AltStore website provides direct download links for these if you need them.

### 3. Install AltStore on your iPhone
1. Plug your iPhone into your computer using your USB cable.
2. If your iPhone asks "Trust This Computer?", tap **Trust** and enter your passcode.
3. **On Mac:** Click the AltServer icon in your top menu bar -> click **Install AltStore** -> select your iPhone.
   **On Windows:** Click the diamond-shaped AltServer icon in your bottom right system tray -> click **Install AltStore** -> select your iPhone.
4. Enter your Apple ID and password when prompted. 
5. Wait a minute, and the **AltStore** app will magically appear on your iPhone's home screen!

### 4. Trust the Developer Profile
Before you can open AltStore on your iPhone:
1. Unplug your iPhone.
2. Open your iPhone's **Settings** app.
3. Go to **General** -> **VPN & Device Management**.
4. Under "Developer App", you will see your Apple ID email. Tap it.
5. Tap **Trust [Your Email]** and confirm.

> **Important for iOS 16+ Users:**
> You must also enable Developer Mode:
> Go to **Settings** -> **Privacy & Security** -> Scroll to the bottom -> **Developer Mode** -> Turn it ON and restart your phone.

### 5. Install the ASync App
1. Send the `app.ipa` file from your computer to your iPhone (you can use AirDrop, email, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive).
2. Open the **AltStore** app on your iPhone.
3. Tap the **My Apps** tab at the bottom.
4. Tap the **+** (plus) icon in the top left corner.
5. Find and select the `app.ipa` file you transferred to your phone.
6. AltStore will ask for your Apple ID one last time to sign the ASync app. Enter it.
7. The ASync app will install and appear in your My Apps list!

### 🎉 The Magic Wi-Fi Auto-Refresh
You can now open ASync and log in. 

To make sure the app **never expires**, just make sure of two things:
1. Leave AltServer running in the background on your computer.
2. Connect your iPhone and computer to the **same Wi-Fi network** occasionally (like when you're sleeping at home).

AltStore will silently communicate with your computer over Wi-Fi and automatically refresh the 7-day timer behind the scenes!
